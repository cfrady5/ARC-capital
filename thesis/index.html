// The Thesis //

Invest where critical technologies converge.

Modern capabilities do not operate in isolation. Biosensors depend on advanced chips. Autonomous systems combine sensing, computation, and action. Biomanufacturing increasingly relies on robotics, data, and intelligent systems.

ARC focuses on the technologies powering this convergence and the companies positioned to translate them into scalable, dual-use capabilities.
